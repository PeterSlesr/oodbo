Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026

Short version: we do not collect anything about you, ever, for any reason. Forward Only runs entirely in your web browser. There is no Forward Only server, no Forward Only account, and no Forward Only database. The people who make Forward Only never receive, store, or have any way to see your writing or any information about you.

What we collect

Nothing. We run no server that receives your data, operate no analytics, set no cookies of our own, show no ads, use no trackers, and have no sign-up or account system of our own. We have no way to identify you or contact you, and we hold no record that you ever used the app.

Where your writing lives

As a guest (not signed in), your writing exists only inside the current browser tab and is gone when you close it. It is never sent anywhere. To keep it, you export it to a file on your own device, or you sign in.

When you sign in with Google, your writing is saved to a private app folder inside your own Google Drive. It goes straight from your browser to your Google Drive — it never passes through, or is stored on, any server of ours. It is encrypted on your device before it is saved, so it does not sit in your Drive as plain, readable text.

How signing in works

Signing in is handled entirely by Google. When you choose to sign in, Google asks your permission and hands your browser a temporary key that lets the app read and write files in your own Google Drive. Your Google password never goes anywhere near us — there is nothing of ours for it to go to. That temporary key lives only in your browser for the session; we never see it or store it. We ask Google only for your email address (so the app can label your account and keep different accounts' work separate) and for access to your Drive.

Sharing

If you choose to publish a share link, the app saves a snapshot of that piece of writing to your own Google Drive and marks it "anyone with the link can view." The shared page is then served to readers from your Drive, by Google — not by us. We do not host it, copy it, store it, or see it. You can remove a share link at any time from the app or directly in your own Google Drive, which takes the page offline. Because a share link is "anyone with the link," treat anything you share as public.

Hosting

The app itself (the HTML and JavaScript files) is delivered by a static hosting provider, Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's infrastructure may keep standard, short-lived technical logs (such as the IP address making a request) purely as part of serving the page. That is Vercel's own infrastructure — it is not data we collect, receive, look at, or use, and there is no account or profile it is attached to.

Storage on your device

To work offline and remember your preferences, the app uses your browser's own local storage on your device (localStorage and IndexedDB). This never leaves your device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data for Forward Only removes it.

Other services

Only two third parties are involved, and only for the purposes above:

  • Google — for optional sign-in and for storing your files in your own Google Drive. Governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Vercel — for hosting the app's files. Governed by Vercel's Privacy Policy.

There are no others — no analytics service, no advertising, no email provider, no payment processor, no database.

Your control

Your writing is always in your hands: it is in your browser or in your own Google Drive, and you can export or delete it at any time. There is no account of ours to close, because there is no account of ours in the first place.

Children

Forward Only is a general-purpose writing tool and is not directed at children, and — since we collect nothing — we do not knowingly or unknowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
